GenZ Keyboard is a complete Android keyboard: it registers with the system, types into any app, and never watches what you type.

TYPING
- 138 built-in layouts, including English variants, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu, Arabic, Persian, Hebrew, Greek, Cyrillic, Chinese pinyin, Japanese and Korean
- Word prediction, autocorrection and next-word suggestions from bundled frequency dictionaries, plus a personal dictionary that learns your words
- Glide (swipe) typing
- Emoji keyboard with search
- Clipboard history, text shortcuts and voice input through the system speech recogniser
- One-handed mode, adjustable height, key size and spacing

LOOKS
- Nine ready-made styles: Material 3 Expressive, Material You, Liquid Glass, Classic, Neon, Neumorphism, Minecraft, Samsung One UI and Nothing
- Ten anime themes, each with its own artwork and a palette sampled from that picture
- Custom themes: background, key colours, accent colour, key shape, shadows and font
- AMOLED dark theme, a light theme, and a one-tap dark mode switch

PRIVACY
- Nothing you type leaves the device. No analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no tracking SDKs
- Settings and learned words stay in local storage
- No account, no sign-in, no network call while you type

OPTIONAL FEATURES
- GIF search and AI text fixing need your own API keys and are off unless you configure them
- Voice typing asks for the microphone permission only when you tap the microphone
